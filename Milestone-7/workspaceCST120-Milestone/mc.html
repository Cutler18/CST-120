Life in the Marine Corps

In 2015 I shipped off to MCRD San Diego to begin my journey with the United States Marine Corps. I spent 3 months learning how to be a Marine through Marine Corps history, rifle training, and intense physical exercise. After that, I spent a month and a half at Camp Pendleton in Marine Combat training before being shipped to Fort Leanordwood, MO for 3 months where I attended my job school for motor transportation. I learned to operate and maintain HMMWV's, 7-Tons, and LVSR's. From there I was sent to Camp Fuji, Japan where I spent a year working as a Motor Vehicle Operator. Japan is also where I injured my shoulder during a hand to hand combat training session. I was later sent to Yuma, AZ where I served the remainder of my time. However, my injured shoulder was never tended to and therefore caused my to be discharged early due to medical reasons. My Marine Corps career was shorter than most, but it taught me a lot and was a foundational point in my life that led to me becoming the man I am today.
